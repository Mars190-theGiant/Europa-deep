The problem

Under ~20 km of ice, Europa hides an ocean.

2×the water of all of Earth's oceans, possibly
15–25 kmof ice shell over an ocean 60–150 km deep
3ingredients for life: water, chemistry, energy

It is one of the best places to search for life. But reaching it is a chain of hard choices: mass, power, cost, travel time and risk. Students read about missions. They rarely get to make those choices.

Fiction for the story. Real data for everything else.

Europa Clipper2024 → 2030
Launched on a Falcon Heavy and flies the Mars-Earth gravity assist. The game offers the same rocket and route.
Galileo1995–2003
Magnetometer data gave the first strong evidence of a salty ocean.

Impact

The game explains why your mission worked, or didn't.

The final report reads the data for you. "Two blackouts in a row. Engineering lesson: a power budget needs margin, just like real spacecraft."

A simulation lab for classrooms. Five ready-made designs, 200 runs each, with charts and a plain-language verdict.

For middle and high school. Academic tone, bilingual, works on phones, and an international crew that includes a wheelchair-using chief engineer.
