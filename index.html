Why it is hard

No choice is free.

Europa Clipper was going to fly on an SLS, straight to Jupiter in under 3 years. NASA switched to a much cheaper Falcon Heavy and accepted flying past Mars and Earth to gain speed: 5.5 years of travel. A bigger rocket costs budget. More cargo weighs more. A long trip wears out the crew. This game puts those trade-offs in your hands.

  1. DesignDestination on the map, rocket, trajectory and crew, within budget and cargo limits.
  2. PlanBuild the base, fit out the submarine, research real Europa Clipper instruments, split the power.
  3. DiveExplore 4 ocean zones, scan discoveries, go out on EVA and decide when the unexpected happens.
